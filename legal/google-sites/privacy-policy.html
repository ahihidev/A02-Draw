AR Drawing

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Short version: AR Drawing does not require an account. Camera frames, drawings, imported images, and most preferences are handled on your device. The App uses online services for its content catalog, Google Images search, and app analytics. This release does not display advertising and does not offer in-app purchases.

1. Scope and contact

This Privacy Policy explains how AR Drawing (the “App”) accesses, uses, stores, and shares information. It applies to the App and not to third-party websites or services that you choose to open.

Privacy contact: wibucodeapp280626@gmail.com

2. Information handled on your device

  • Camera: If you grant camera permission, live camera frames are processed while an AR camera feature is open. The App does not use the camera in the background. Photos and videos you capture are saved to your device. The developer does not upload your live camera feed or captured media to its own server.
  • Images and creations: Images selected through the Android system picker, images selected from the in-app browser, generated Emoji Mix images, and temporary working files may be copied to App-private storage so they can be displayed, edited, traced, saved, or shared. Exporting or sharing occurs only when you choose a destination or another app.
  • Drawings and preferences: Saved drawings, favorites, lesson progress, language, onboarding status, sound settings, and similar preferences are stored locally in the App’s database or preferences storage.
  • Clipboard: When you explicitly use a copy action, the App may write the selected result or file reference to the Android clipboard. The App does not use this feature to read unrelated clipboard content.

3. Information processed through online services

Content catalog and image downloads. The App connects over HTTPS to obtain drawing lessons, categories, reference metadata, and other App content. The server or its hosting provider may receive standard network data such as your IP address, request time, App version, and technical request information. When you select an online image, the App downloads the image from its source URL. Camera media and your local drawings are not sent with these catalog requests.

Google Images and Android System WebView. If you choose the Web Browser feature, your search query is sent to Google Images through an embedded Android WebView. Google and WebView may process your IP address, cookies or site storage, browser and device information, search terms, and page interactions under Google’s Privacy Policy. Avoid entering personal or sensitive information in search queries.

Google Analytics for Firebase. The App enables analytics through the Kiro SDK and Google Analytics for Firebase. Depending on your device and settings, analytics may process an App-instance identifier, App opens and interactions, session statistics, App and device information, operating-system information, approximate location inferred from IP, diagnostic information, and an Android advertising identifier when available. We use this information to understand feature usage, maintain performance, diagnose problems, and improve the App. The App does not require your name, email address, phone number, or contact list for analytics. Learn more about Google Analytics data safeguards.

4. Advertising and in-app purchases

This release does not request or display banner, native, interstitial, or rewarded ads, and it does not offer subscriptions or other in-app purchases. Advertising and Google Play Billing components may remain packaged in the App for a possible future release, but the current App does not request ad inventory, show purchase offers, or launch a payment flow. If these features are enabled later, this Policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before or with that release.

5. How information is used

Information described in this Policy is used only as reasonably necessary to:

  • provide camera, drawing, lesson, search, import, save, export, and sharing features;
  • remember settings and progress on your device;
  • deliver online content and download images you select;
  • measure App usage, maintain reliability, prevent abuse, and improve features;
  • comply with law, enforce rights, or protect users and the App.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be disclosed or made available:

  • to Google Analytics/Firebase and Kiro as technology providers for the purposes above;
  • to Google, WebView, and a source website when you use online search or download an image;
  • to the app, person, or storage destination you select when you export or share content;
  • to infrastructure providers that host App content or process technical requests;
  • when reasonably required by law, legal process, security, fraud prevention, or a business transfer subject to applicable notice and safeguards.

7. Retention and deletion

Local drawings, imported references, generated files, preferences, and progress remain on your device until you delete them, clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App. Files exported to shared storage or another app may remain after uninstalling and must be deleted from that destination separately. WebView cookies and site data are controlled by Android System WebView and your device settings. Google and other service providers retain information according to their own policies and configured retention periods.

The App does not create a user account, so there is no server account to delete. To remove local data, use Android Settings → Apps → AR Drawing → Storage → Clear storage, then delete exported files separately. For a privacy request concerning information under the developer’s control, email us at the address above. We may need enough information to understand and lawfully respond to the request, but we will not ask for unnecessary identity documents.

8. Your choices and rights

You may deny or revoke camera permission, avoid the Web Browser feature, clear WebView or App data through Android settings, reset or delete the Android advertising ID where supported, and decide whether to export or share a file. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent. These rights may be subject to legal exceptions. Contact us to exercise an applicable right.

9. Children’s privacy

The App does not provide accounts and does not knowingly ask children for names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or payment details. A parent or guardian should supervise a child’s use of camera, web search, downloading, and sharing features. If you believe information relating to a child has been processed improperly, contact us so we can review the issue and take appropriate action. The App’s declared target audience in Google Play must remain consistent with the released App and its enabled SDK configuration.

10. Security and international processing

The App uses Android permission controls, App-private storage, and encrypted HTTPS connections for supported network traffic. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Google, hosting providers, and other service providers may process information in countries other than yours, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.
